Use Malbit together

  1. Open this page in Chrome if possible. Share your screen or use Connect phone to show your captions on your friend’s device.
  2. Choose the language you’ll speak, select Start microphone, and allow access.
  3. Speak in short phrases. The Korean text updates as recognition comes in and may change until the phrase is complete.

Use the expand button for a reader-only view, or Type a message if your microphone is unavailable.

Speech and translation providers

Your browser may send audio to its speech recognition service. Chrome can translate on your device when its language pack is available. Otherwise, Malbit sends recognized or typed text to MyMemory, which has a free daily usage limit. A live session also sends recent Korean captions to Malbit so connected devices can read them until the session ends or expires.
